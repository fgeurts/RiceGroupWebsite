## On Campus

You can find Herman Brown Hall looking for building #17 at [this map](http://www.rice.edu/maps/maps.html) (see blue arrow).

The nearest visitor parking is available at the North Annex lot accessible via Entrance #20 (Rice Boulevard). Other parking lots that are available to off-campus visitors are the West Lot (opposite of the Alice Pratt Brown Hall, #15 on the map — entrances 8 or 18) and the Greenbriar lot (west of the Rice Stadium — entrances 13A or 13B). Parking rates differ and for more information, including Accessible Parking, please refer to [this link](https://parking.rice.edu/Content.aspx?id=32). For the more remote parking facilities, visitors can make use of the free shuttle service. A live tracker is available at [this link](http://bus.rice.edu/).
