The following workshops and conferences have been organized (in part) by the Rice Heavy Ion Group.

* [Fluctuations 2012](http://macfrank.rice.edu/Workshops/FluctuationsWorkshop/Welcome.html)  
  STAR Topical Meeting on Fluctuations & Particle Identification
* [MTD/eTOF 2013](http://macfrank.rice.edu/Workshops/MTD2013/)  
  STAR MTD & Endcap TOF Workshop
* [THIS 2017](http://macfrank.rice.edu/Workshops/THIS2017/)  
  Texas Heavy-Ion & Spin physics symposium
* [eTOF 2018](http://macfrank.rice.edu/Workshops/etof2018/)  
  STAR/CBM eTOF Workshop
* [HardProbes 2020](https://indico.cern.ch/event/751767/)  
  10th edition of the Hard and Electromagnetic Probes International Conference
* [TSAPS 2022](https://tsaps22.rice.edu)  
  Texas Section APS/AAPT Meeting
* [STAR PreQM 2023](https://macfrank.rice.edu/Workshops/STARpreQM23/)  
  STAR Pre-QuarkMatter Approval meeting
* [QuarkMatter 2023](https://indico.cern.ch/e/QM2023)  
  XXXth International Conference on Ultra-relativistic Nucleus-Nucleus Collisions, co-organized by UH, TAMU, and Rice
