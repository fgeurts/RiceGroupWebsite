# Local Group Resources

On this page you will find several resources that are of use to our group's activities. For other resources, look in the upper submenu.

* [STAR@Rice mailing list](https://mailman.rice.edu/mailman/private/staratrice-l/)
  News and updates for Rice folks who are involved in the STAR experiment at the Relativistic Heavy-Ion Collider (RHIC) at Brookhaven National Lab.

* [Rice Heavy Ions Slack](https://rice-star.slack.com)
  Our collaboration/chat tool.

* [Rice Heavy Ions @github](https://github.com/RiceU-HeavyIons)
  Direct link to the RiceU-HeavyIons github organization.

* [Heavy-Ions Journal Club](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/2PACX-1vRJKHVoLrXxeIAxkhJy_7NjpMyaqtjSQEWsitX-LruoYp0H6WrfkorL0DeqDnDKraOercOVg4ri--cQ/pubhtml)
  Past contributions and upcoming paper discussions.

* [Local STAR file server](https://macfrank.rice.edu/STAR)
