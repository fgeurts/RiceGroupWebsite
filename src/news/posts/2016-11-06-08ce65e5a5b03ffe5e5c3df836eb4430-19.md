<div class="image-left"><img class="imageStyle" alt="Pasted Graphic" src="/news/files/pasted-graphic-2.jpg" width="309" height="264"></div>The STAR Analysis Meeting, scheduled for November 10 - 12, 2016 has been announced. This meeting will see in-depth discussions on the results that the collaboration prepares for the upcoming QuarkMatter conference. It also will see discussions on the status of several of the papers that it plans to release in the near future. Three of those papers (including dielectron measurements in the RHIC Beam Energy Scan, and Nuclear Modification Factors for ideatedied particles from the RHIC Beam<div class="image-right"> </div>Energy Scan) involve analyses and principle authors from the Rice group.

Link to the registration page: <a href="https://sites.google.com/lbl.gov/star2016">https://sites.google.com/lbl.gov/star2016</a>
