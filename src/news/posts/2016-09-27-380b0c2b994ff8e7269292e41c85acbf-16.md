---
title: "Hard Probes 2020: the Texas Edition"
date: 2016-09-27
---

Earlier this week, the International Advisory Committee of the 8th International Conference on Hard and Electromagnetic Probes of High-Energy Nuclear Collisions (Hard Probes 2016) announced that the 10th installment of this important conference series will be held in 2020 at UT Austin and involves the collaborative effort of the four main heavy-ion institutes in that region: UT Austin, Texas A&amp;M, Univ. of Houston, and our group at Rice University
