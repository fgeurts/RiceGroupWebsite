title: "STAR Collaboration Meeting in Cracow"
date: 2019-08-23
---

In August the AGH UST group hosted the STAR Collaboration Meeting in Cracow, Poland. The organizers should be congratulated on a perfectly organized meeting (with excellent coffee!) as can be seen from the many smiles on the obligatory collaboration meeting picture. It was a well-attended meeting with many familiar faces from across the globe. I look forward returning to Cracow for the 2021 QuarkMatter conference!
