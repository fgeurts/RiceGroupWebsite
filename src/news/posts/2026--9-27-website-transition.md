---
title: "Rice Heavy Ion Group Website transition"
date: 2026-08-27
---

The Rice Heavy Ion Group website (yes, the one that you are looking at now) has undergone an important transition. For any of our viewers nothing (should have) changed. But, under the hood we migrated from RapidWeaver's framework to a newer environment based on Eleventy. This will make future maintenance much easier, as well as provide native version control through a dedicated git repository.

Much of the transition involved long and patient discussions between FG and his new friend Gemini. We did not always agree, but the end result looks pretty good.
