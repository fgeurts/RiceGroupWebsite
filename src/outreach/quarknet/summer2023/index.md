<h1>QuarkNet Summer Workshop at Rice University 2023</h1><br /><br />The 2023 Summer Workshop will be held at Rice University from June 19 - 23, 2023. The agenda for this workshop is posted below (and may change a little bit). The links will give more specific details on some of workshops components. Days start at 9am and end at 4pm. There will be presentations from Rice and UH faculty right before (or after) lunch, except on Friday where we will start off with a presentation and activity.<br /><br /><br /><img class="imageStyle" alt="F784DCDF-1DC5-42C1-982E-486445730114_1_105_c" src="/outreach/quarknet/summer2023/files/f784dcdf-1dc5-42c1-982e-486445730114_1_105_c.jpg" width="536" height="408" /><br />Photo caption: QuarkNet 2023 participants and fellows next to STAR's event display of a central Au+Au collision. Also, make sure sure that you provide the relevant information for the payment to the organizers.
