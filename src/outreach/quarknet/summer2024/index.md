<h1>QuarkNet Summer Workshop at Rice University 2024</h1><br /><br />Funded by NFS, through Notre Dame University, Quarknet is a week-long $120/day paid stipend 30-credit hour workshop that allows &ldquo;<em>teachers and physicists to work together on physics research projects exploring the hidden nature of matter, energy, space, and time.</em>&rdquo;  The 2024 Summer Teacher Workshop will be held at Rice University from June 10 - 14, 2024. The agenda for this workshop is posted below (and may change a little bit). The links will give more specific details on some of workshops components. Days start at 8h30am and end at 3h30pm. There will be presentations from Rice and UH faculty right before  lunch.<br /><br /><h3>Registration</h3><br /><br />The Department of Physics & Astronomy at Rice will provide for daily lunches and parking vouchers. Teachers will receive a stipend for the days that they attend, so it is important that you sign the sign-in/out sheet when you arrive/leave. Also, make sure that you provide the relevant information for the payment to the organizers. Finally, we will provide a certificate to all who have completed the 5-day workshop, certifying their professional development.<br /><br /><h3>Logistics (Getting there, parking there, eating there, &hellip;)</h3><br />The workshop will take place at Rice University, Brockton Hall  room 103. To get to the campus, please take a look at <a href="/contact/rice/" title="How to get to Rice">this link "How to get to Rice</a> and to get around campus we have some instructions at <a href="/contact/campus/" title="On campus">this link "How to get around campus."</a>  The map, below, shows where to find Brockman Hall (marked as building #72) with respect to the North Parking Lot. Look for entrances 20 or 21 to get to the North Parking Lot. Be prepared to use your credit card to enter the lot.
