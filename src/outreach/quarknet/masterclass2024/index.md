<h1>QuarkNet Master Class 2024</h1><br /><br /><br /><br />We are excited to host you all on our campus for the upcoming Quarknet Masterclass (Saturday March 2). Below I provide a few more details on how to get to Rice, where to park, what room were are in, and what the agenda will look like. If you have not yet made us aware of your preference, then please contact Mary Yarbray or me before Thursday COB. We will make sure that there will be a mix of options, including vegetarian.<br /><br /><h2>Parking</h2><br />The best place to park will be the North Parking lot which can reach through Entrance #20 (for more parking options, see http://geurts.rice.edu/contact/campus/). We provide a limited amount of parking vouchers to those students/parents who carpool.<br /><br /><h2>Location</h2><br />We will use the computer lab of Duncan Hall 1020. This room is accessible from outside Duncan Hall. Campus tour 1 will be at Brockman Hall 120. Campus tours 2a,b will both be in the basement of Brockman Hall. All Brockton Hall tours are only accessible under guidance of a Rice professor or graduate student. <br /><br /><h2>Agenda</h2><br />The agenda involves a couple of lectures on particle physics and the analysis of real data from the LHC. We will also walk the students to a nearby lab in which we have set up an experiment in which we determine the lifetime of a muon.
