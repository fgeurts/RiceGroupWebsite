<h1>QuarkNet Summer Workshop at Rice University 2026</h1><br /><br />Funded by NFS, through Notre Dame University, Quarknet is a four-day-long $480 paid stipend 30-credit hour workshop that allows &ldquo;<em>teachers and physicists to work together on physics research projects exploring the hidden nature of matter, energy, space, and time.</em>&rdquo;  The 2026 Summer Teacher Workshop will be held at Rice University from June 15 - 18, 2026. The agenda for this workshop is posted below (and may change a little bit). The links will give more specific details on some of workshops components. Days start at 8h30am and end at 3h30pm.
