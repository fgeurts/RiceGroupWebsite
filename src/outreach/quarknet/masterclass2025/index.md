<h1>QuarkNet Master Class 2025</h1><br /><br /><a href="/resources/QuarkNet/2025/Masterclass-at-Rice-U.pdf"><img class="imageStyle" alt="Masterclass at Rice U" src="/outreach/quarknet/masterclass2025/files/masterclass-at-rice-u.png" width="886" height="499" /></a><br /><br />We are excited to host you all on our campus for the upcoming Quarknet Masterclass (Saturday March 29). Below we provide a few more details on how to get to Rice, where to park, what room were are in, and what the agenda will look like. <br /><br />Our Masterclass will take place on Saturday, March 29, from 8h30 am until about 4 pm. <br /><br /><h2>Registration</h2><br />Registration is free. Please use <a href="https://forms.gle/XsHWgj75CoKUH2By6">this link to access the registration form</a>. <br />Teachers: (1) please contact Frank Geurts as soon as possible to make sure that we have teacher chaperons for each school that plans on participating; (2) also, reach out to Barbara Braun or me in order to receive a stipend for your attendance (to be confirned).<br /><br /><br /><h2>Lunch</h2><br />The Physics & Astronomy department has graciously offered to cover the lunch. If you have not yet made us aware of your preference, then please contact Mary Yarbray or me before Thursday COB. We will make sure that there will be a mix of options, including vegetarian.<br /><br /><h2>Parking</h2><br />The best place to park will be the North Parking lot which can reach through Entrance #20 (for more parking options, see http://geurts.rice.edu/contact/campus/). We provide a limited amount of parking vouchers to those students/parents who carpool.<br /><br /><h2>Location</h2><br />We will use the computer lab of Duncan Hall 1020. This room is accessible from outside Duncan Hall. Campus tour 1 will be at Brockman Hall 120.<br /><br /><h2>Agenda</h2><br />The agenda involves a couple of lectures on particle physics and the analysis of real data from the LHC. We will also walk the students to a nearby lab in which we have set up an experiment in which we determine the lifetime of a muon.
