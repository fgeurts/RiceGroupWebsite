<h1>QuarkNet Summer Workshop at Rice University 2025</h1><br /><br />Funded by NFS, through Notre Dame University, Quarknet is a week-long $480 paid stipend 30-credit hour workshop that allows &ldquo;<em>teachers and physicists to work together on physics research projects exploring the hidden nature of matter, energy, space, and time.</em>&rdquo;  The 2025 Summer Teacher Workshop will be held at Rice University from June 9 - 13, 2025. The agenda for this workshop is posted below (and may change a little bit). The links will give more specific details on some of workshops components. Days start at 8h30am and end at 3h30pm. There will be presentations from Rice and UH faculty right before  lunch.<br /><br /><h3>Registration</h3><br /><br />Register has closed.
